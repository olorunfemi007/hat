The list above was captured just before this hotspot started and will not update while it's running — the helmet's Wi-Fi radio can't scan for networks and run a hotspot at the same time. If your network isn't listed, use the manual field instead.

After you submit, {{.APSSID}} will disconnect while the helmet attempts to join your network — that's expected, not an error. If this page doesn't come back within about a minute, reconnect your phone/laptop to {{.APSSID}} and reload this page: either it worked (and the hotspot is gone for good), or you'll see an error here and can try again.

iPhone/iPad: if this page didn't open automatically after joining the hotspot, open Safari and browse to any http:// address to bring it back (a known iOS issue can prevent the automatic prompt).
